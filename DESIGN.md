# Design System: Strata

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Survey Sheet"**

Strata reads like a daylight survey sheet laid beside a live instrument. The measured ground and the bridge are the subject; everything around them is matte paper on a cool instrument-grey ground, ruled with one hairline, set in a plain grotesque and a firm geometric display face. Figures read as a legend, never as a stat row: each carries its status as a dot and a word, and its source as a chip.

The interface is quiet so the scene can lead. One warm colour, the survey ochre, marks the one action on each stage and the live year on the timeline; every other selection is drawn in ink. Nothing is glass, nothing floats on a shadow, nothing semi-transparent sits over the bridge. Motion answers a change of state with a colour change and stops there; all of it is off under reduced motion.

This is a light-only system (`themes: [light]`): the use scene is a visitor in daylight, often on the spot or in a classroom. There is no dark mode.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Matte opaque panels with one 1 px hairline: no blur, no shadow, no glass.
- Survey ochre only for the one action and the live year mark; selected state is ink.
- Status as a dot plus its word: measured, text-attested, inferred.
- Geologica 600 display, Public Sans 400 and 600 body, self-hosted from @fontsource.
- Radii control 4, panel 8, and full round only for chips and round marks.
- Tabular numerals wherever a number can change.

## Colors

A cool instrument grey carries the page, paper carries the chrome, ink does the work, and one survey ochre marks the action.

### Primary
- **Survey Ochre** (#A67C00, `accent`): the fill of the one primary button on each stage (Take the guided tour on the landing and in the help card, Play the 60 s story on the place card, Next in the guide card, Watch the film when WebGL is missing) and the thumb of the year slider, the live year mark. Its hover is the lighter **Lit Ochre** (#C79E42, `accent-400`). Text on it is **Night Ink** (#101214, `on-accent`).
- **Ochre Text** (#725400, `accent-text`): the only ochre that may colour text, because it passes AA on paper and ground. Used today for the hover of the landing's text links.

### Secondary
- **Survey Blue ramp** (`support-50` to `support-950`): the complementary cool ramp the palette generator writes. No chrome uses it; it exists for the blue end of the height ramp in the scene.

### Neutral
- **Instrument Grey** (#EAEDF3, `bg`): the page ground, the landing's copy column, the loading screen, and the quiet fill of inputs, source chips and secondary buttons.
- **Survey Paper** (#F4F7FC, `surface`): every panel, the dock, the controls, the credit line, modals, the Reimagine backdrop, and text on ink buttons.
- **Hairline** (#D7DBE1, `border`): the one 1 px rule on every panel, row and field. The same step (`neutral-200`) is the hover fill and the sky behind the canvas.
- **Strong Hairline** (#BDC1C9, `neutral-300`): hover borders, status-chip edges, the slider track, the sheet handle.
- **Drawing Ink** (#292B30, `ink`): text, the selected state of every toggle, legend item and thumbnail, the dark secondary buttons (Ask, Play the 60 s story in the controls), and the focus ring. Its hover is `neutral-800` (#41444A).
- **Pencil Grey** (#565961, `muted`): secondary text, captions, credits, labels, and the inferred status dot.

### Status
- **Measured Green** (#007B36, `success`): the measured dot.
- **Attested Blue** (#006EAE, `info`): the text-attested dot.
- **Inferred Grey** (`muted`): the inferred dot, with a dashed chip edge.
- **Refusal Red** (#AF3D36, `danger`): the docent's refusal chip, a source id that does not resolve, a voice error.
- **Warning Umber** (#915B00, `warning`): defined, unused in the chrome today. It sits near the ochre's hue, so when it appears it carries an icon and a label.

### Named Rules
**The One Ochre Rule.** Survey ochre fills one control per stage and the live year thumb. A second ochre button on a screen is a bug.

**The Ink Selects Rule.** A selected view, toggle, legend item, thumbnail or part box turns ink with paper text. In the app's short names, `--accent` is ink; the survey ochre is `--color-accent`.

**The Dot And Word Rule.** A status is never a colour alone. The dot always sits beside its word.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geologica 600 (with sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Public Sans 400 and 600 (with sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** none; the system monospace only where code shows

**Character:** Geologica is a firm geometric display face that names things: the wordmark, the place, the part. Public Sans is a civic grotesque that reads like a survey's labels and carries everything else. Both are self-hosted from @fontsource (`public-sans/400`, `public-sans/600`, `geologica/600`); nothing loads from a font CDN.

### Hierarchy
- **H1** (Geologica 600, 39px, 1.1): the landing title, Strata. It drops to the h2 size on phones.
- **H2** (Geologica 600, 31px, 1.2): the place name on the place card. It drops to the h3 size on phones.
- **H3** (Geologica 600, 25px, 1.3): the selected part's name in the inspector, the Reimagine heading, the loading line.
- **Lead** (Public Sans 400, 20px, 1.4): the landing's one line, in Pencil Grey, at most 30rem wide; 16px on phones.
- **Body** (Public Sans 400, 16px, 1.5): panel text, docent answers, controls. Body never goes below 16px on phones.
- **Label** (Public Sans 600, 16px, 1.3): figures in the landing legend, button labels, the year.
- **Caption** (Public Sans 400, 13px, 1.5, 0.01em): chips, credits, legend notes, section labels (set 600 in Pencil Grey, sentence case, never upper case).

The wordmark in the panel head is Geologica 600 at the label size, after an 8px mark.

### Named Rules
**The Two Weights Rule.** Public Sans ships 400 and 600 only. A weight of 500 renders as 400.

**The Tabular Rule.** Every number that can change (year, bascule angle, hydraulics, figures) is set in tabular figures.

## Layout

**The landing (Home stage).** A two-column grid: the copy on the Instrument Grey column at the left (360px to 34rem wide, centred vertically, 32px padding, 24px between blocks), and the turning bridge in its own band at the right on the sky grey, divided by one hairline. The copy runs: the title, the one line, the actions (the ochre button, then the text links Enter Tower Bridge and Play the 60 s story), the figures as a survey legend (hairline rows, the figure in a 7.5rem column, then a status chip and a muted note), and one caption line naming the places coming next. Below 860px it becomes one column: the bridge in a clear 38svh band at the top with a hairline under it, the copy and button below, the page scrolls.

**The app.** The canvas runs full bleed under the chrome. The panel is a 380px opaque side sheet along the left edge, inset 16px; the dock is a strip along the bottom to the right of the panel, at most 760px wide; the view and toggle controls stack in a 232px column at the top right; the credit line sits at the top, right of the panel. Cards opened over the stage (help, no WebGL, loading) centre in the space right of the panel, so the subject stays clear of the panel.

**Phones and tablets (900px and below).** The panel becomes a bottom sheet, collapsed to 148px (the handle, the wordmark and search) on first load and opening to 62svh. The dock sits on top of the sheet, the controls wrap into a row under the credit line, the inset drops to 8px and the panel padding to 16px. Touch targets are 44px.

**Spacing.** A 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 scale. The panel's inner padding is 20px on desktop; groups inside it are separated by 24px and a hairline.

## Elevation & Depth

Strata is flat. There are no shadows and no blur: the app's `--shadow` and `--blur` are both `none`. Depth comes from two tonal steps and a rule: Survey Paper sits on Instrument Grey, and every panel is closed by one hairline. Over the scene, the panel, the dock, the controls and the credit are fully opaque. The one full-cover state (Reimagine, no WebGL, loading) uses the same opaque paper, never a dimming scrim.

The only box-shadow in the system is the focus ring's paper halo: a 2px ink outline, offset 2px, with a 2px Survey Paper ring behind it so it reads over the scene. The only fade is a 24px mask at the foot of the panel's scrolling list, which says the list scrolls.

### Named Rules
**The Matte Paper Rule.** Anything over the live scene is opaque paper with one hairline. If you can see the bridge through it, it is wrong.

## Shapes

Three radii, each with one job. **Control** (4px): buttons, inputs, segmented buttons, the close button, thumbnails, photos and tooltips. **Panel** (8px): the panel, the dock, the controls group, the credit line, modals, the search results, the figures table and the hydraulics box. On phones the sheet rounds its top corners only. **Full round** (999px): chips, and the small round marks: status dots, the wordmark's mark, the voice dot, slider thumbs, the help card's step numbers and the sheet handle.

Edges are a single 1px hairline, never doubled. The inferred status chip alone has a dashed edge. The year slider's track is a 2px square rule with tick marks under it.

### Named Rules
**The Round Is A Chip Rule.** Full round belongs to chips and dots. A button or input is never a pill. In the app's short names `--pill` is the 4px control radius; the true full round is `--radius-pill`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently squared (4px).
- **Primary** (`.primary`): Survey Ochre fill, Night Ink label in Public Sans 600 16px, 44px tall, 24px side padding, an optional 12px play glyph. Hover lightens to Lit Ochre. One per stage.
- **Ink:** Drawing Ink fill with paper text, 40px tall (44px on touch): Ask, Play the 60 s story in the controls, Fly to where you stood and the photo upload in Identify, retry on loading. Hover is `neutral-800`.
- **Quiet:** paper or grey fill, one hairline, ink text (Reimagine, Identify, the help button). Hover strengthens the hairline or the fill.
- **Text link** (`.home-link`): ink, 600, underlined with a 4px offset, 44px tall. Hover turns Ochre Text.

### Segmented controls
- **Style:** the view switch (orbit, top-down, walk) and the toggles (exploded, LiDAR) sit in an 8px paper group with a hairline; each button is 32px (44px on touch), ink text on no fill.
- **State:** hover fills Hairline grey; selected is an ink fill with paper text.

### Chips
- **Status chip** (`.chip.status`): 24px tall, full round, a Strong Hairline edge, a caption-size ink word after an 8px dot coloured by status. Inferred has a dashed edge. The refusal chip ("No source, so no answer") has a red dot and red word.
- **Source chip** (`.chip.source`): grey fill, hairline, ink title, linking to the source. A source id that does not resolve shows in red.
- Chips are 44px tall on touch where they are tappable.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 8px.
- **Background:** Survey Paper, opaque.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** one 1px Hairline.
- **Internal Padding:** 12px 16px for small chrome; 20px inside the panel; 24px for the help card and modals.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** search and ask are 40px tall (44px on touch), 4px corners, a grey fill and one hairline, placeholder in Pencil Grey.
- **Focus:** the edge strengthens and the fill lightens to Hairline grey; keyboard focus adds the ink ring with its paper halo.

### Navigation (the dock)
- **Year slider:** a 2px Strong Hairline track, the 14px Survey Ochre thumb (24px on touch) as the live year mark, the year above it in label weight and tabular figures, the current event in Pencil Grey, ellipsised, and the city source as a caption at the right.
- **Ticks:** 1px ink marks at 45% for sourced events, 2px solid ink for build stages; hover, focus or the current mark grows to 11px, and a small paper tooltip names it.
- **Bascule slider:** the same track with an ink thumb, its angle in tabular figures.

### The inspector
The selected part's group as a section label, its name in H3, a summary, then the figures table: one hairline box, rows divided by hairlines, the label in Pencil Grey at the left, the value at the right in tabular figures with its unit, and under it the status chip and the source chip. Before a part is chosen, the parts list shows as wrapped buttons (4px, grey fill, hairline). The hydraulics box adds live simulated values, marked inferred.

### The docent
Pinned to the foot of the panel under a hairline. Your question sits right-aligned on a Hairline-grey tile; the answer is body text with its source chips below it, or the refusal chip. Thinking is three Pencil Grey dots that pulse; a live answer shows a blinking ink caret. Both stop under reduced motion. The ask row is the input, an ink Ask button and a voice toggle that turns ink when on.

### Identify and Reimagine
Identify boxes each named part on the photo with a 2px paper outline and a paper name tab; hover or focus outlines a box in Survey Ochre, and the chosen box turns ink. Reimagine opens on an opaque paper backdrop: a paper modal (8px, hairline) with a before-and-after slider over the photograph, a 2px paper divider and small dark tags naming each side.

## Flow

1. **Landing.** The Home stage: the turning bridge in its band, the name, one line, and the figures as a survey legend, each with its status dot and word.
2. **Take the guided tour.** The one ochre button. Enter Tower Bridge and Play the 60 s story sit beside it as text links.
3. **The scene.** The bridge on its measured ground, full bleed; the opaque panel at the left, the dock along the bottom, the view controls at the top right. The guide card steps through the tour with an ochre Next.
4. **A part.** Tap a part on the bridge or in the panel's list; the inspector shows its figures, each with a status chip and a source chip.
5. **The year.** Drag the year slider in the dock; the ochre thumb is the live year mark and the ticks mark events and build stages.
6. **The docent.** Ask at the foot of the panel; the answer comes with source chips, or a calm refusal chip when it cannot cite one.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** give Survey Ochre to one control per stage and to the live year thumb, nothing else.
- **Do** make every panel, dock, card and modal opaque Survey Paper with one 1px Hairline.
- **Do** show a status as a dot plus its word: measured in green, text-attested in blue, inferred in grey with a dashed edge.
- **Do** set names in Geologica 600 and everything else in Public Sans 400 or 600, self-hosted from @fontsource.
- **Do** use 4px for controls, 8px for panels, and full round only for chips and round marks.
- **Do** set every changing number in tabular figures and read figures as a legend: figure, status, source.
- **Do** keep the height ramp (deep blue to teal to ochre) where it encodes height on the point cloud.
- **Do** write plain verbs and numbers with their source: "North tower, 65 m, text-attested."

### Don't:
- **Don't** use ochre for a selected state, a toggle, a tick or decoration; selected is ink with paper text.
- **Don't** put blur, glass, transparency or a shadow over the scene; the focus ring's paper halo is the only box-shadow.
- **Don't** show a figure without its status and source, or a status dot without its word.
- **Don't** reach for weight 500 or a font CDN: only 400 and 600 ship, so 500 renders as 400.
- **Don't** round a button or input into a pill; the short name `--pill` is the 4px control radius.
- **Don't** lay numbers out as a row of stat tiles or put a card over the bridge.
- **Don't** put a gradient on a button, heading, background or divider.
- **Don't** write superlatives or "Oops! Something went wrong."; say what cannot be done and what still works.
